Case study

Controller-managed Wi‑Fi (CAPsMAN)

MikroTikCAPsMANVLAN-aware SSIDsfleet provisioning

Scale two controller stacks in parallel (legacy 802.11n/ac + newer ax) · dozens of managed APs · corporate, guest-isolated, and segregated SSIDs on separate VLANs

Situation

Manufacturing campus Wi‑Fi mixed new ax access points with legacy gear. One-off AP configs did not scale and made “what changed?” unanswerable.

Task

Move toward controller-driven provisioning so new APs join with the right SSIDs/VLANs and ops reviews happen on the manager, not every edge.

Approach

  1. Treat CAPsMAN (or wifi wave2 controller stack) as the source of truth for managed WLANs.
  2. Validate VLAN tagging end-to-end (SSID → VLAN → DHCP → firewall).
  3. When many CAP exports change after a controller edit, classify them as sync echo unless local bridge/ether config moved.
  4. Document join/re-provision steps for the next AP drop-in.

Result

  • Repeatable AP onboarding; Wi‑Fi changes reviewable at the controller.
  • Clear ops rule: don’t invent fifteen “AP tickets” for one controller commit.
